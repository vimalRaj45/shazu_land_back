Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 10, 2026

1. Information Collection

Shazu Soft Technologies collects basic informational logs upon student registration, newsletter opt-in, or membership applications. This is limited to candidate names, university affiliations, email coordinates, and contact telephony numbers.

2. Usage of Data

Collected details are strictly used to coordinate training groups, assign conference paper reviewer schedules, deliver accreditation check dashboards, or send newsletter mailings. We do not distribute database listings to outer corporate entities.

3. Security Standards

We implement industry-standard computer security layouts to prevent unauthorized database read access. Access is restricted to key recruitment coordinators and digital operations officers in Salem.

Shazu Soft Technologies reserves the right to modify this document. Any adjustments are published directly on this website. Continuance of membership implies approval of active guidelines.
