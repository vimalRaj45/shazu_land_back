Membership Categories

P

Professional Member

For engineering professionals, college lecturers, and journal authors. Entitles members to join peer-review committees, submit patent advisories, and get conference pricing discounts.

S

Student Member

For undergraduate and research candidates. Grants priority access to certified internship programs, regional hackathon applications, and mentoring sessions.

I

Institutional Member

For universities and engineering colleges. Incorporates continuous accreditation data reviews (NBA/NAAC checklists) and coordinates joint campus seminars.
