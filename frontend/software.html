CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers about agreements, code ownership, and how we collaborate.

Do we own all source code and intellectual property?

Yes, 100%. Upon final delivery and settlement, you receive complete ownership of all repositories, architecture diagrams, Figma design tokens, and deploy keys. We never lock you into proprietary vendor frameworks.

Will you sign an NDA before we share our project brief?

Without hesitation. We routinely execute mutual Non-Disclosure Agreements prior to preliminary scoping calls to ensure your business models, trade secrets, and logic remain strictly confidential.

How do we monitor progress throughout development?

We work in transparent 2-week agile sprints. You receive private staging preview links, regular video walkthroughs, and direct communication with the project lead via Slack or WhatsApp.

What happens after launch? Do you provide ongoing maintenance?

We provide 30 days of warranty support post-launch, alongside ongoing SLA maintenance retainers for cloud infrastructure monitoring, security updates, and new feature development.
